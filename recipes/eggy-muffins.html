Eggy Muffins

The perfect easy breakfast!

This recipe is both delicious and super easy to make. All you need is a muffin tin, eggs, toppins of your choice, and 30 min.

Future you will thank yourself for making these all week. The perfect, nutritious, start to your day awaits!

Ingredients

  • One dozen (12) eggs
  • One package of breakfast sausage links
  • Chopped green onion for topping

Steps

  1. In a medium sized skillet, cook the sausage until done.
  2. Cut the sausage links into small rounds.
  3. In a large mixing bowl, crack open all of the eggs.
  4. Whisk eggs until fully scrambled
  5. Add the sausage rounds into a pre-greeased muffin tin. Try to put an even amount in all of the cups.
  6. Pour the eggs into the muffin cups. Be careful not to overfill the cups by aiming to get them 3/4 full.
  7. Sprinkle the green onion on top of the cups.
  8. Bake at 350 degrees for 25 to 30 minutes
  9. Once the eggy-muffins are cooked through, remove them from the oven let them rest
  10. Put the eggy-muffins a storage container (or containers).

Thats it! Easy right?
